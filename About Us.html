About Fungitech

FUNGITECH MUSHROOMS is dedicated to producing high-quality, organic mushrooms using modern technology. We empower farmers and provide healthy, nutritious food to communities across Zimbabwe. From spawn production to cultivation training, we are your one-stop mushroom solution.

It is also committed to creating the optimal environment for mushroom farmers to thrive and succeed. Our mission is to empower farmers with the knowledge, resources, and support needed to build and grow prosperous mushroom farming businesses.

Grey Oyster Mushroom

Grey oyster is one of the world's most widely cultivated
gourment mushrooms, valued for its vigous growth, excellent yields
and adaptability to a wide range of growing conditions.

Ideal For

  • Commercial mushroom farms
  • Small-scale growers
  • First-time cultivators
  • Fresh market sales

Growing Characteristics

  • Fast colonisation
  • High biological efficiency
  • Reliable and consistent yield
  • Excellent contamination reistance
  • Perfoms well on straw, juncao, hardwood sawdust and other cellulose-rich substrates

Fruiting Temperature

12-20°C

Flavor

Rich, savoury and meaty with excellent umami flavour
